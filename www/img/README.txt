RECIPE PHOTOS — not wired up yet
================================
Recipe cards are currently text only, by design: an abstract icon tells you
nothing about how a dish looks, so the space went to the name and the numbers.

When you have real photos, put them here and ask me to switch them on. I'll
add the image layer back to the cards and the recipe page, match each file to
its recipe, and compress them.

Naming: lowercase, spaces and punctuation become hyphens, max 48 characters.
  "Sheet Pan Chicken Pitas with Tzatziki" -> sheet-pan-chicken-pitas-with-tzatziki.jpg
The full list of expected filenames is in slugs.txt next to this file.

Recommended: 800x600 or larger, JPEG, under 150 KB each. They ship inside the
APK, so 173 uncompressed photos would bloat the download badly.

Licensing: the recipes are adapted from pinchofyum.com and their photographs
are their copyright. Shoot your own, or use a source whose licence permits
commercial use.
